PRIVACY POLICY — SOKOFY

Last Updated: 1 February 2026

1. Introduction

Busara Digital (“Busara Digital”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Sokofy, a simple Point-of-Sale (POS) and business management application designed to assist shopkeepers and business owners—such as retail shops, wholesale businesses, and service-based businesses—in managing day-to-day operations, including sales records, business summaries, and operational insights (the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose information when you use Sokofy through our mobile applications, associated web services, and related features.

Sokofy is currently intended for users in the United Republic of Tanzania. We plan to expand availability to other countries, including Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, Rwanda, and additional regions. When required, this Privacy Policy will be updated to comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR-style privacy principles and similar regulations.

By accessing or using Sokofy, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must discontinue use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, secure, and improve the Service.

2.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use Sokofy, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Shop or business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials (passwords are never stored in plain text)

2.2 Device and Technical Information

To ensure proper functionality, security, and support, we may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device unique identifier (UID)
  • Device platform (Android or iOS)
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • IP address (where required for security or network operations)

When users submit bug reports or feature requests, certain technical details may be included automatically in messages sent via our support system (SMTP email), solely for diagnostics and service improvement.

2.3 Usage Data

We may collect anonymized or aggregated usage data, including:

  • App interaction events
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Error logs and crash diagnostics

This data does not directly identify individual users and is used only to improve the Service.

2.4 Payment Information (Future Feature)

At the current MVP phase, Sokofy does not process payments and does not collect payment or billing data.

If payment or subscription features are introduced in the future, users will be notified in advance, and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

3. How We Use Information

3.1 Service Operation

  • To store and process business data directly on the user’s device
  • To calculate sales totals, summaries, and operational insights
  • To deliver POS and business management functionality

3.2 Authentication and Security

To authenticate users via email-based login and Google Sign-In. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication, which applies industry-standard security practices.

3.3 Analytics and Performance Improvement

To understand app usage trends and growth. To improve stability, features, and user experience. Analytics are collected using Firebase Analytics in aggregated form.

3.4 Support and Communication

To respond to support requests via:

  • WhatsApp
  • Email: sokofy.pos@gmail.com

To troubleshoot issues and respond to feature requests.

3.5 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

  • To comply with applicable laws and lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, safety, and property of users and Busara Digital

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Sokofy does not sell, rent, or trade personal user data.

4.1 User-Controlled Sharing

Users may voluntarily export or share their own business data (for example, generating or sharing PDF reports). Such actions are fully controlled by the user.

4.2 Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services strictly for app functionality:

  • Firebase Authentication – secure user login and identity management
  • Firebase Analytics – app usage analytics

These services process data in accordance with their own privacy and security policies.

4.3 Legal Obligations

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order, or lawful government request.

5. Data Storage, Backup, and Security

5.1 Local Data Storage

Business operation data is primarily stored locally on the user’s device using a secure local database. Deleting the app or account permanently removes locally stored data.

5.2 Cloud Services and Future Backup

User authentication data is securely managed by Firebase Authentication. In future versions, optional cloud backup or synchronization features may be introduced to help users protect their data.

Any such feature will be clearly explained, optional, and covered by an updated Privacy Policy.

5.3 Security Measures

Passwords are encrypted and managed securely by Firebase. Access controls and technical safeguards are applied. We take reasonable measures to protect data, though no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. User Rights (GDPR-Aligned Principles)

Users have the right to:

  • Access their data within the application
  • Update or correct their information
  • Delete their account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Once data is deleted from the device, it cannot be recovered.

7. Application Access and Updates

Sokofy is distributed exclusively through Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Updates may be released regularly to improve features, performance, and security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Sokofy is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If such data is identified, it will be deleted promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or services, legal or regulatory requirements, and operational or security improvements.

Updates will be communicated through app store listings, in-app notices, or updated policy pages.
